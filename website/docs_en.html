1. Overview

ViewStereo is a native macOS studio application for automatically detecting, rectifying, and restoring 19th-century historical stereoview cards (stereographs) as well as modern stereo photographs into Apple Vision Pro Spatial Photos (MV-HEVC), Side-by-Side (SBS), Anaglyph, MPO, and digitally remounted cards.

Early Development Notice: ViewStereo (v0.1.1) is currently in active early development. All Core ML models are bundled directly inside the app and run 100% offline on your Mac.

2. Basic Workflow

  1. Import Media: Drag and drop a scanned stereoview card, a Left/Right image pair, an SBS image, or a folder onto the canvas (or press ⌘O).
  2. Auto Segmentation & Alignment: When enabled, ViewStereo automatically runs Core ML card segmentation, 7-DoF homography alignment, color matching, and stereo window placement (re-run manually with ⇧⌘A).
  3. Inspect Preview: Switch between 8 display modes via the top toolbar or ⌘1–⌘8. Press X to swap Left/Right views if needed.
  4. Fine-Tune or Remount: Adjust STEREO WINDOW horizontal shift or customize STEREOVIEW CARD MOUNT presets.
  5. Export: Choose your target Stereo Layout in the footer and press ⌘E.

3. Core ML Card Segmentation

Configure card extraction under BASIC RECIPE > STEREO CARD DETECTION:

  • Card Mode:
    • Off: Skips card segmentation for modern L/R pairs and pre-cropped SBS images.
    • Crop Inner: Removes the cardboard mount and extracts clean rectangular L/R prints (recommended).
    • Keep Arch (Black): Preserves vintage arch-top silhouettes against a black mask.
  • Core ML Card Segmentation (AI): Uses StereoCardSeg (U-Net) to localize left/right prints and estimate mount luminance.
  • Segmentation Refiner: Choose between MobileSAM (fast & lightweight), SAM 2.1 Tiny (high precision for aged borders), or U-Net Only.
  • Card Frame: Apply classic Off, Rounded, or Arch borders to modern stereo photos.

4. 7-DoF Homography & MAAIR

Historical hand-mounted prints frequently suffer from roll tilt, magnification mismatch, keystoning, and vertical disparity. ViewStereo’s StereoCore solver uses Apple Accelerate LAPACK (dposv_) + IRLS to symmetrically rectify both views and eliminate vertical disparity to sub-pixel precision.

  • Vision Person Motion Mask (Cha-Cha): Masks moving human subjects in handheld Cha-Cha captures so static backgrounds align accurately.
  • Keep Original Aspect Ratio (MAAIR): Computes the Maximum Area Axis-aligned Inscribed Rectangle across both warped views to guarantee zero black borders.

5. Stereo Window Calibration

To prevent uncomfortable frame-edge window violations, ViewStereo automatically calculates the optimal horizontal shift (Auto: XX.X px) from foreground disparity.

  • Vision Saliency Subject Priority: Prioritizes the primary subject detected by Apple Vision Attention Saliency when placing the stereo window.
  • Horizontal Shift (px): Real-time 3-piece studio control (slider, reset button, and numeric input from -300.0 to +300.0 px).

6. Digital Remount & OCR

Select Card (Stereoview Card) in the display mode or export layout to reconstruct museum-grade stereoview cards.

Era-Authentic Mount Styles (8 Presets)

  • Original Mount (Remount) & Auto Sample Source Color
  • 1900s Keystone Dark (charcoal slate curved mount)
  • 1880s Classic Buff (warm buff mount)
  • 1870s Cabinet Terracotta & 1870s Victorian Green (with period bicolor edges)
  • 1860s Chrome Yellow & 1850s Early Ivory (square corners & prints)

Specs, Print Cuts & Letterpress OCR

Supports 7-inch card standards (Standard 7 × 3.5 in, Cabinet 7 × 4.0 in, Imperial 7 × 4.5 in), print cuts (Arched Top, Rounded Corners, Square), and center division styles. With Letterpress (Auto OCR), Vision OCR reads catalog numbers, captions, and vertical publisher credits from the original scan and re-typesets them cleanly.

7. Color Harmonization

  • Auto Color Match: Choose Off, Left Ref, or Right Ref to harmonize exposure differences and uneven sepia aging between left and right prints.
  • Remove Unilateral Noise & Dust: Suppresses dust specks and scratches that appear in only one eye’s view.

8. Display Modes & Export

Switch seamlessly between 8 real-time display modes (2D, SBS, LR/RL, Dubois, Color Anaglyph, Gray Anaglyph, Polarized, Card) and export across 11 layouts, including Separate L/R, Spatial Photo (HEIC / MV-HEVC) for Apple Vision Pro, and MPO (3D Camera). Adjustment reports (.txt) can also be saved.

9. Batch Folder Processing

Press ⇧⌘B or drop a folder directly onto the canvas to batch-process entire archives. Supports Auto (L/R Pairs + SBS), SBS Images Only, and Consecutive L/R Pairs, outputting by default to <Input>/ViewStereo_Output.
