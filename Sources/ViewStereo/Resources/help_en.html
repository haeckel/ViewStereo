ViewStereo Help

ViewStereo is a professional macOS studio application for automatically segmenting, rectifying, and optimizing 19th-century stereoviews (stereographs / stereo cards) and modern stereo photographs into pristine digital 3D media, including Apple Vision Pro Spatial Photos and Side-by-Side (SBS).

Key Features

  • Stereoview Card Auto-Segmentation: Effortlessly detects and separates left/right photograph areas and vintage arch-top frames from scanned stereo cards using Core ML (StereoCardSeg + SAM 2.1 Tiny).
  • 7-DoF Symmetric Homography Optimization: Solves scale, roll, horizontal/vertical keystoning, and vertical disparity down to sub-pixel accuracy via LAPACK dposv_ + robust IRLS.
  • Intelligent Stereo Window Alignment: Automatically places the stereo window behind the nearest foreground subject, eliminating window violations and retinal rivalry.
  • MAAIR (Maximum Area Axis-aligned Inscribed Rectangle): Accurately computes and crops to the largest common rectangular area without black borders.
  • Vintage Restoration & Color Harmonization: Compensates for exposure imbalances, uneven aging, and dust between left and right lenses.
  • Direct Spatial Photo (HEIC) Export: One-click export to Apple Vision Pro Spatial Photos, Side-by-Side (SBS), Anaglyph, and Wiggle 3D formats.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • ⌘O: Open Stereo Card, L/R pair, or SBS image
  • ⇧⌘A: Auto Align & Window Calibration
  • X: Swap Left / Right images
  • ⌘1 / ⌘2 / ⌘3: Switch between Alignment, Window, and Export tabs
  • ⇧⌘B: Batch Process Folder
